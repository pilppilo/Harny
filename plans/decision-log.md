# Architecture decision log

Append decisions in chronological order. Do not rewrite earlier entries;
supersede them with a new entry that links back to the prior decision.

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Context:** What forced the decision?
- **Decision:** What was chosen?
- **Consequences:** What becomes easier, harder, required, or excluded?
- **References:** Relevant plans, schematics, issues, commits, or research.
